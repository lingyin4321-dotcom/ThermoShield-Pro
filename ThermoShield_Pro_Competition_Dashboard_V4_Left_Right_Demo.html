🧠 Hysteresis Logic

≤27°C → Fan OFF 🟢 → 27–29°C → Maintain Previous State 🟢🟡 → 29–32.9°C → Fan ON (Warm) 🟡 → ≥33°C → Fan ON (Crit) 🔴
The 27–29°C hysteresis zone prevents frequent ON/OFF switching and keeps operation stable.
